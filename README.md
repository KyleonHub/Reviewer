# Reviewer - RLW & Mixed Signals

An interactive, responsive, and mobile-optimized multi-subject reviewer built with dark mode and engineering simulations.

## 📚 Subjects Included

### 1. Mixed Signals (FMSS)
- **Interactive Component Workbench**:
  - **Op-Amp Circuit Lab**: Interactive circuit simulator for Inverting, Non-Inverting, Voltage Follower, and Comparator with real-time sliders ($, {in}$, {in}$, $\pm V_{cc}$) and live dual-trace oscilloscope with rail saturation and ^\circ$ phase inversion.
  - **741 IC Pinout Explorer**: Interactive DIP-8 chip package with detailed pin inspection and absolute maximum ratings.
  - **Sensor Signal Chain**: 5-stage architectural pipeline simulator (Ultrasonic, Temperature LM35, Strain Gauge Bridge, Flame Sensor).
- **Randomizer Quiz**: 104 technical questions, circuit computations, gain formulas, and matching rounds.
- **Flashcards**: Golden rules, pinouts, and key formulas.

### 2. Rizal's Life and Works (RLW)
- **Randomizer Quiz**: 167 randomized questions (MCQ, True/False, Identification, Matching).
- **Flashcards**: 65 comprehensive flashcards with local historical imagery.

## 📱 Mobile Optimized
- Thumb-friendly touch targets (≥48px).
- iOS Safari auto-zoom prevention.
- Fluid typography and responsive layout.

## 🚀 Getting Started
Open index.html directly in your browser, or double-click START_REVIEWER.bat on Windows.
